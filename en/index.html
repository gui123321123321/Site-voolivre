How it works

From the first contact to the flight.

You do not need to know how everything works when you arrive. We guide you through every step so you know what to expect before coming to Pedra Bonita.

01

Get in touch

You let us know via WhatsApp which days you are available to fly.

02

We find the best time

The team monitors the weather and wind conditions and advises you on the best day and time to fly.

03

Meeting and registration

On the agreed day, you meet the team at the Clube São Conrado de Voo Livre headquarters and complete the necessary registration before heading to Pedra Bonita.

04

Flight preparation

The equipment is prepared and checked. Before takeoff, the pilot explains the safety procedures, takeoff, landing, and the instructions you must follow.

05

Flight and landing

When conditions are suitable, takeoff is authorized. The pilot accompanies you throughout the flight until landing at Praia do Pepino.

The sky decides too

Not every day is a flying day.

Free flight depends on weather and wind conditions. That is why the team monitors the forecast and local conditions to advise you on the best time to fly.

Flight conditions are monitored before takeoff. If the minimum requirements for the flight are not met, takeoff is not authorized. In that case, the flight can be rescheduled for a more favorable time.

Before flying

A few things you need to know.

For your experience to go as smoothly as possible, there are some important details to consider before the flight.

Never flown before?

No previous experience is required. Before takeoff, the pilot explains the procedures and instructions you must follow.

Age

The activity is intended for people aged 18 and over. Minors aged 14 and up may fly with a parent or legal guardian present during registration.

Weight

There is no single weight limit for all flights. The possibility of taking part depends on the pilot, the equipment, and the flight conditions.

Footwear

Closed-toe footwear is preferred. Sandals or flip-flops may only be used if they are securely fastened to your feet.

Your belongings

Small personal items can be stored in the equipment compartment. Backpacks and larger items are carried by a team member to the landing site.

How to get there

The meeting takes place at the Clube São Conrado de Voo Livre headquarters. Directions to the exact location are sent via WhatsApp when you make your booking.

Safety and preparation

Before flying, everything needs to be ready.

Preparation is part of the flight. The equipment, local conditions, and procedures are checked before takeoff.

01

Equipment prepared

The equipment is assembled and prepared according to the procedures for the activity before takeoff.

02

Equipment checked

After assembly, the necessary checks are carried out before the flight can be cleared.

03

Instructions before takeoff

The pilot explains the safety procedures, takeoff, landing, and the instructions to follow during the activity.

04

Conditions monitored

Weather and wind conditions are monitored before and during the takeoff preparation process.

05

Takeoff clearance

Takeoff is cleared when the conditions required for the flight are present.

06

If conditions are not sufficient

If the conditions required for the flight are not present, takeoff is not authorized and the flight can be rescheduled.
